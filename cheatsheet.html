Reference 01 · Strict Upload Portal KB & MB Limits

Government, Passport, Visa, Exam & ATS Resume Specifications

Strict portals reject uploads that exceed exact byte limits. You can target exact sizes with ImageMagick/Ghostscript CLI or browser-based binary-search compressors like Vexelloom Image Compressor and PDF 1MB Compressor.

Application / Portal Type Recommended Dimensions Strict File Size Cap Accepted Format Engineering Notes
Digital Signature & Thumb Impression 140 × 60 px (or 300 × 120 px) 10 KB – 20 KB JPG / JPEG Tightly crop white margins & use grayscale quantization
Passport, Visa & Exam ID Headshot 200 × 230 px or 600 × 600 px (1:1) 20 KB – 50 KB JPG (Plain Backdrop) Strip EXIF metadata & target Q70–Q78 (50KB preset)
E-Commerce Product & Core Web Vitals LCP 1080 × 1080 px or 1200 × 900 px ≤ 100 KB WebP / AVIF / JPG Serve via <picture> with fetchpriority="high"
Blog Hero & OpenGraph Social Card 1200 × 630 px (1.91:1) or 1920 × 1080 px ≤ 200 KB WebP / JPG Use Q78–Q82 perceptual sweet spot
ATS Job Resume, CV & Cover Letter PDF A4 (210×297mm) or US Letter ≤ 1 MB (1,024 KB) PDF (Selectable Text) Downsample embedded bitmaps to 150 DPI without rasterizing text
Visa Packet, Academic Transcript & Tax PDF Multi-page A4 / Letter (150–200 DPI) ≤ 2 MB (2,048 KB) PDF Compress embedded scans before merging pages
